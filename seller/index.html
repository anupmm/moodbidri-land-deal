Conventions

Land and stamp duty are paid at month 0. Development spends from its start month through start + duration − 1; sales follow the same convention, then three buffer months. Approval duration is a planning check, not an automatic delay.

Monthly interest = opening loan balance × annual rate ÷ 12, capitalized before draws and repayment. Sale proceeds after brokerage repay debt first. Annual IRR compounds the monthly return. Hurdle solvers use equity NPV at the target rate, so they still work when IRR is undefined.

Our tax line is a modelling assumption from the brief (12.5% × 1.15 surcharge × 1.04 cess ≈ 14.95% on the gain above basis), not a determination of liability. The buyer's 1% TDS is an advance credit against it, not a second tax. No buyer income tax, GST or holding costs are modelled.
